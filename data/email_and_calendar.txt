EMAIL & CALENDAR
================

Issue: Outlook is not syncing new email
-----------------------------------------
1. Check the bottom bar: if it says "Working Offline", go to Send/Receive and turn off Work Offline
2. Close Outlook fully and reopen it
3. If new mail is still missing after 30 minutes, remove your account and add it again
4. Still stuck? Contact it-support@company.com with a screenshot of the status bar

---

Issue: Mailbox is full
------------------------
- The mailbox limit is 50 GB per person
- Empty the Deleted Items and Junk folders first
- Use Archive to move anything older than 1 year into your online archive
- If you are still over the limit, request a temporary increase at it-portal.company.com

---

Issue: Calendar events show the wrong time
--------------------------------------------
1. In Outlook go to Settings → Calendar → Time zone and pick your current zone
2. Make sure your computer's own time zone setting matches
3. Invites from other offices arrive in the organizer's time zone and convert automatically once yours is correct
4. After travelling, restart Outlook so it picks up the new zone

---

Issue: Shared mailbox does not appear in Outlook
--------------------------------------------------
- Request access at it-portal.company.com → Access Requests → Shared Mailbox
- After approval it can take up to 60 minutes to show up in your folder list
- Restart Outlook if it has not appeared after an hour

---

Setting up out-of-office replies
----------------------------------
1. In Outlook choose File → Automatic Replies
2. Select Send automatic replies and set the start and end dates
3. Write one message for people inside the company and a separate one for external senders
4. Name a colleague who can help while you are away

---

Issue: Email bounced as "message too large"
---------------------------------------------
- The attachment limit is 25 MB per message, including all files
- For anything larger, upload the file to OneDrive and share the link instead
- Zipping a file rarely helps if it is already compressed, such as video or PDFs
