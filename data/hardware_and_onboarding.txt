NEW EMPLOYEE SETUP & HARDWARE
=============================

Issue: Setting up a new laptop on your first day
------------------------------------------------
1. Power on the laptop and connect to the CorpWiFi network or a wired connection
2. Sign in with your company email and the temporary password from your welcome email
3. Set a new password of at least 12 characters when prompted
4. Enroll in MFA with an authenticator app when asked
5. Wait for the device management agent to install the standard software; this takes 20-40 minutes and the laptop restarts twice
6. If setup stalls for over an hour, open a ticket with it-support@company.com

---

Issue: Requesting a replacement laptop or a broken device
---------------------------------------------------------
Company laptops are refreshed every 3 years. For a broken or failing device, open a ticket with the category Hardware and say what is wrong.
1. IT will try a remote fix first
2. If it cannot be fixed, a loaner is shipped within 2 business days for remote employees, or available same day at the IT desk in the office
3. Back up files to company cloud storage before sending the device back, because it will be wiped
4. Return the old device within 14 days using the prepaid label in the box

---

Issue: Ordering a monitor, keyboard, mouse or headset
-----------------------------------------------------
1. Open the Self Service portal and choose Request Hardware
2. Pick items from the approved catalog; standard items are approved automatically
3. Items over 300 dollars need manager approval
4. Delivery takes 3-5 business days; remote employees receive items at their home address
5. Home office furniture is not ordered through IT; use the home office stipend instead

---

Issue: Laptop battery drains quickly or the laptop overheats
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Check Task Manager or Activity Monitor for apps using high CPU, and quit them
2. Switch the power mode to Balanced or Battery Saver
3. Keep the laptop on a hard, flat surface so that the vents are not blocked
4. Reduce screen brightness and close unused browser tabs
5. If the battery health shows below 80 percent, open a Hardware ticket for a battery replacement

---

Issue: Laptop is lost or stolen
-------------------------------
1. Report it to it-support@company.com and security@company.com within 1 hour
2. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device
3. Change your password from another device
4. If it was stolen, file a police report and give the report number to IT

---

Issue: Docking station or external monitor not detected
-------------------------------------------------------
1. Unplug the dock power and cable, wait 30 seconds, and reconnect the power first, then the laptop cable
2. Try a different cable or port on the laptop
3. Press Windows key plus P (or open Displays on a Mac) and choose Extend
4. Update the dock firmware from the Self Service portal
5. If one monitor still fails, test it with another laptop and report the result in your ticket
