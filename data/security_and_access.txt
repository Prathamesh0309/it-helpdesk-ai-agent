SECURITY & ACCESS
=================

Issue: I received a suspicious email
--------------------------------------
1. Do not click links or open attachments
2. Use the Report Phish button in Outlook, or forward the message as an attachment to security@company.com
3. If you already clicked a link or entered your password, change your password straight away at accounts.company.com/reset and call ext. 4357

---

Issue: Laptop lost or stolen
------------------------------
1. Report it within 1 hour to security@company.com and call ext. 4357
2. IT will lock the account and remotely wipe the device
3. For theft, also file a police report and send the report number to Security
4. A replacement is issued after the report is logged

---

Issue: New phone and MFA codes stopped working
------------------------------------------------
- MFA uses the Microsoft Authenticator app, and codes do not move to a new phone automatically
- Go to accounts.company.com/mfa and re-register the new phone using one of your backup codes
- If you have no backup codes, IT verifies your identity through your manager, which takes up to 4 hours

---

Issue: Alert about a sign-in from an unrecognized location
------------------------------------------------------------
- This alert means your account was used from a place or device we have not seen before
- If it was you, for example while travelling, no action is needed
- If it was not you, choose "This wasn't me", reset your password immediately and tell security@company.com

---

Issue: USB drives and external storage are blocked
----------------------------------------------------
- USB storage is blocked by default to prevent data loss
- Use OneDrive to move files instead
- If you have a business need, request an exception at it-portal.company.com with your manager's approval

---

Issue: Laptop asks for a disk encryption recovery key
-------------------------------------------------------
- Windows laptops use BitLocker and Macs use FileVault
- After signing in with MFA, find your recovery key at it-portal.company.com/recovery
- Never share the recovery key with anyone, including IT staff who contact you unprompted
